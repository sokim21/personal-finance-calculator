Advanced tax assumptions & limitations

Federal tax uses a simplified 2026 progressive schedule and standard deduction, with preferential capital gains bands. Illinois uses an illustrative flat rate. Other states require a manually entered estimated rate. Does not fully model multistate sourcing, state credits, AMT, QBI, rental passive-loss rules, NIIT, phaseouts, child/dependent credit eligibility, capital loss carryforwards, or bonus withholding. Tax credits are user-entered estimates. The displayed tax is an annualized liability estimate, not exact paycheck withholding.

Version 3.4 · Educational prototype, not tax or individualized financial advice. Assumptions are simplified and editable. The early-career allocation is an illustrative heuristic, not an optimization or a class-mandated priority order. Minimum payments are treated as required cash outflows. The retirement model assumes constant nominal returns and inflation, constant real spending and benefits starting at retirement, end-of-year contributions, and end-of-year withdrawals; it ignores taxes, benefit timing, fees, market volatility, healthcare shocks, and account-specific rules. Do not enter sensitive account information.
