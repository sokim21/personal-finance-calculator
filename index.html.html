Educational prototype, not individualized financial advice. Assumptions are simplified and editable. The early-career allocation is an illustrative heuristic, not an optimization or a class-mandated priority order. Minimum payments are treated as required cash outflows. The retirement model assumes constant nominal returns and inflation, constant real spending and benefits starting at retirement, end-of-year contributions, and end-of-year withdrawals; it ignores taxes, benefit timing, fees, market volatility, healthcare shocks, and account-specific rules.
